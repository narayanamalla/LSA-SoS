# Week 1: Governing Equations & Special Flow Conditions

## 🎯 Weekly Goal
Master the fundamental equations of fluid motion, understand the core simplifications (2D, steady, incompressible), and derive classical viscous flow solutions.

## 📚 Key Topics Covered
* **Fundamental Flows:** * Poiseuille Flow (pressure-driven flow between parallel plates).
  * Couette Flow (drag-induced flow).
* **Navier-Stokes Simplifications:** Applying assumptions to simplify mass and momentum conservation equations.
* **Introduction to Boundary Layers:** The concept of the thin boundary layer and an introduction to the Blasius solution.

## 📝 Mentee Activity
**Task:** Derive the velocity profile for Poiseuille flow from the generalized mass and momentum conservation equations.

* **Instructions:**
  1. Start with the full 2D, incompressible Navier-Stokes equations.
  2. Apply the assumptions for fully developed, steady flow.
  3. Integrate the simplified differential equation.
  4. Apply the no-slip boundary conditions at the walls to find the constants of integration.
  5. Plot the resulting parabolic velocity profile (optional: use Python/MATLAB).
